Thomson Reserve Launch: Prices, Cash and Income Needed, With a Calculator

By Dan Ler, Mortgage Advisor

Short answer

Thomson Reserve is priced from S$2,608 psf. Starting prices are S$1.544M for a two-bedroom, S$2.503M for a three-bedroom and S$3.388M for a four-bedroom. Previews opened on 17 October and booking day is 31 October 2026, with TOP expected in the second half of 2030. On the starting two-bedroom, a Singapore Citizen buying a first home needs S$77,200 in cash on booking day, about S$355,600 in cash and CPF by the S&P stage including stamp duty, and roughly S$10,050 a month in income to pass the bank's stress test. Use the calculator below for your own unit and profile.

Cash and Stamp Duty by Unit Type

The 5% booking fee is the only part that must be cash on the day. The stress test decides the rest.

A new launch is paid in stages under the progressive payment scheme. The first 20% comes from you before the bank lends anything:

  • 5% booking fee when you take the option to purchase on booking day. This part must be cash.
  • 15% within eight weeks of the option date, when you exercise the sale and purchase agreement. Cash, CPF, or both.
  • Buyer's Stamp Duty within 14 days of the S&P, and ABSD in the same window if it applies.

On a first housing loan at 75% LTV, the remaining 5% of the downpayment is collected at the first construction stage, and the bank starts paying the developer after that. For a Singapore Citizen buying a first property, the starting price of each type works out like this:

ABSD comes on top for anyone who is not a Singapore Citizen buying a first home. On the same three starting prices it is S$308,800, S$500,600 and S$677,600 for a Citizen's second property at 20%, and S$77,200, S$125,150 and S$169,400 for a PR's first property at 5%. Plan for ABSD in cash: IRAS expects it within 14 days, and paying it from CPF depends on your CPF balance and the CPF Board's rules, often by claiming it back afterwards.

The Income Each Unit Needs

The bank does not test you at the rate you will pay. MAS requires it to work out your instalment at the higher of your actual rate and 4% a year, then check that all your monthly debt repayments, including this one, stay within 55% of gross income. That is the TDSR limit, and it decides more purchases than the price does.

Those figures assume no other debts and a 30-year tenure. Two things push them up. Any car loan, card balance or personal loan instalment comes off the same 55%. And the tenure must end by age 65 to keep the 75% LTV, so a borrower aged 40 gets 25 years, not 30, and a higher instalment on the same loan. The calculator handles both.

HDB Upgraders: ABSD and the Second Loan

Thomson Reserve will draw a lot of HDB upgraders, and they face two separate issues that are easy to mix up.

ABSD. If you keep your flat while you buy, Thomson Reserve is your second residential property. A Singapore Citizen pays 20% ABSD: S$308,800 on the starting two-bedroom. A married couple with at least one Singapore Citizen who buy jointly can claim it back if they sell the flat within six months of Thomson Reserve's TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier. TOP is expected in the second half of 2030, so that is a long runway, but you still need the ABSD in hand at the S&P stage. Check your eligibility against IRAS's conditions before you book.

The second loan. ABSD is about how many properties you own. The loan limit is about how many housing loans you still owe. If your HDB flat still has a loan, HDB or bank, the Thomson Reserve loan is a second housing loan: the maximum LTV drops from 75% to 45%, and at least 25% of the price must be cash. On the S$1.544M two-bedroom that means a loan of S$694,800 and at least S$386,000 in cash, before stamp duty. Clearing the HDB loan before you book changes that completely, which is why the order of steps matters. Our HDB-to-condo upgrade guide walks through the sequencing.

Instalments start small and step up sharply at TOP, when a quarter of the price is drawn at once.

The Rate During Construction

Loans for uncompleted properties are almost always floating, priced off SORA or a bank's own board rate. Fixed packages are generally sold on completed homes. So for Thomson Reserve the decision until 2030 is not fixed or floating; it is which floating package, and on what terms:

  • The spread and how it steps up. A low first-year spread that jumps in year three matters less here, because the drawn balance is small in the early years.
  • The lock-in. Ideally it ends before TOP, so you are free to reprice or refinance when the full loan is drawn and fixed packages become available.
  • Valuation. New launches are valued off the developer's own pricing, and banks do not all value the same unit the same way. A lower valuation means a smaller loan and more cash from you.

Our fixed vs floating guide covers the choice you will face at TOP, and today's SORA is on our rates page.

Before Booking Day

  • Get an In-Principle Approval for the loan size you need, ideally from more than one bank. It confirms your TDSR and loan limit before you commit the booking fee. See how an IPA works.
  • Have the 5% ready in cash, usually as a cashier's order, for the unit price you are aiming at, plus some headroom for a higher floor.
  • Check your CPF Ordinary Account balance and how much of the next 15% and the stamp duty it can cover. Private property has CPF usage limits tied to valuation and remaining lease.
  • If you own an HDB flat, decide now whether you will sell before or after TOP, and whether any HDB loan will be cleared first. That decides your LTV and your ABSD.
  • Put any car loan or card debt into the sums. Every dollar of monthly debt reduces the loan you can get.
  • Know your walk-away price. Set it from the calculator before the preview, not at the sales table.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of Thomson Reserve?

From S$2,608 psf. Starting prices are S$1.544M for a 592 sq ft two-bedroom, S$2.503M for a 947 sq ft three-bedroom and S$3.388M for a 1,238 sq ft four-bedroom. Those are the cheapest units of each type; the full list is out for booking day on 31 October 2026.

When does Thomson Reserve launch, and when is it completed?

Previews began on 17 October 2026 and booking day is 31 October 2026. TOP is expected in the second half of 2030, with vacant possession expected by 28 February 2031.

How much income do I need to buy a two-bedroom at Thomson Reserve?

At S$1.544M with a 75% loan over 30 years, the bank's 4% stress test gives an instalment of about S$5,528 a month, which needs about S$10,052 of gross monthly income under the 55% TDSR, before other debts.

How much cash do I need on booking day?

The 5% booking fee in cash: S$77,200 on the starting two-bedroom. A further 15% follows within eight weeks in cash or CPF, plus stamp duty within 14 days of the S&P. For a Citizen's first home that is about S$355,600 by the S&P stage.

I own an HDB flat. Can I buy at Thomson Reserve?

Yes, once the flat has met its MOP. Expect 20% ABSD as a Citizen buying a second property, refundable to eligible married couples who sell the flat within six months of TOP or CSC. If the flat still has a loan, the new loan is capped at 45% LTV with at least 25% in cash.

Can I take a fixed rate on a Thomson Reserve loan?

Not usually during construction. Loans for uncompleted properties are generally floating. Fixed packages become available on completed homes, so the time to switch is around TOP.

Who is the developer of Thomson Reserve?

Tamarind Development Pte. Ltd., a joint venture of CapitaLand Development, UOL Group and Singapore Land Group, redeveloping the former Thomson View.
